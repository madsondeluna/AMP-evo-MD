# Selective and lytic sequences were verified against UniProt. The inactive class is designed
# scrambled controls: one per active peptide (6 active, 6 inactive, composition-matched and
# balanced). Each scramble is the permutation of its parent that minimises the Eisenberg

# (scram-magainin1, scram-melittin, scram-V681) by tools/make_scrambles.py (seed per parent).
# The "binds without lysing" class is intentionally omitted (no clean alpha-helical example
# under the imposed-helix model).
